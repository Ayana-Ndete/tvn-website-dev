Sarit Centre, Nairobi · 2026Live

Sarit Belong Loyalty Management System

A mall-wide loyalty and e-purse platform connecting shoppers, merchants and partners. Earn points at any store, spend them anywhere, and pay with M-Pesa.

Customer engagementPayments & integrationsDigital commerce
Project details

Sarit Belong brings the Sarit Centre loyalty programme into one digital experience. Shoppers use iOS and Android apps to earn and spend points, hold value in an e-purse, redeem vouchers and pay for parking. Merchants, partners and the centre team each have their own portal to run promotions and campaigns and to see how the programme is performing.

Behind the apps is a complete points economy. Points are earned at participating merchants and redeemed across the mall and at off-mall partners. Merchant billing and redemption reimbursements are settled automatically through the centre's ERP. The platform was designed to comply with Kenya's Data Protection Act from the start.

Key capabilities

  • iOS and Android customer app
  • Points and e-purse ledger
  • Promotions engine, vouchers, referrals
  • Customer segmentation for campaigns
  • Merchant and partner portals
  • Parking payments
  • Admin portal and reporting

Technology & integrations

  • M-Pesa payments
  • NetSuite ERP: automated billing and reimbursements
  • Infobip OTP and SMS
  • Power BI reporting
  • Keycloak single sign-on with session controls

Our role

Technical leadership, solution architecture and delivery of the apps, back office and integrations.

the dtic, South Africa, with TradeMark Africa · 2026Live

DTIC Export Barrier Reporting & Resolution (NTB) Platform

A national platform where exporters report trade barriers by web, mobile, SMS or WhatsApp in four languages. AI routes each case to the right agency and tracks it through to resolution.

Trade facilitationPublic-sector systemsAI & data intelligence
Project details

Exporters facing non-tariff barriers (unexpected rules, delays or charges at a border or in a destination market) need a simple way to report them, and confidence that someone will act. We built a national platform for South Africa's Department of Trade, Industry and Competition that gives them both.

Exporters, including SMEs and informal traders, can report in English, isiZulu, Kiswahili or French. Each report becomes a case with configurable service levels, and can be referred to the Tripartite and AfCFTA mechanisms. AI classifies each complaint, routes it, suggests resolution paths from past outcomes, and spots recurring problems before they spread.

Key capabilities

  • Multi-channel, multilingual reporting
  • Guided reporting for SMEs and informal traders
  • AI classification (Tripartite/AfCFTA categories) and routing
  • Case management with SLAs and escalation
  • AI-assisted resolution recommendations
  • Hotspot, trend and risk-alert intelligence
  • Analytics dashboards

Technology & integrations

  • Kubernetes in government data centres, autoscaling, defined SLOs
  • Highly available PostgreSQL with backup and DR
  • Row-level security and data masking
  • WTO ePing, ITC Market Access Map, WhatsApp Business API, SMS, email

Our role

Technical leadership, architecture, development, deployment, stakeholder validation, national training and change management.

Zanzibar Food and Drugs Agency · 2024–2027Live

ZFDA Integrated Regulatory Management Information System (IRMIS)

A rebuilt national regulatory system for foods, drugs, cosmetics and medical devices, with public and internal portals and government payments.

Regulatory technologyPublic-sector systems
Project details

Under a three-year contract, we rebuilt ZFDA's regulatory system. We replaced an end-of-life platform with a modern, secure one, without disrupting live regulatory services. Businesses apply and pay online through the public client portal (portal.rims.zfda.go.tz). ZFDA staff process applications on the internal portal (rims.zfda.go.tz). Go-live was phased, starting with the client portal in November 2024.

We redesigned product registration by product type, and added bulk permit uploads, digital pharmacist IDs with self-service renewal, and payment through ZanMalipo, the Zanzibar government payment gateway.

Key capabilities

  • Public client portal: applications, renewals, payments
  • Internal staff portal with zone-based access (incl. Pemba)
  • Premises, product, GMP, pharmacist and import/export modules
  • Bulk Excel permit upload
  • ZanMalipo payment integration
  • Management dashboards and public transparency analytics

Technology & integrations

  • Java 6 → Java 21 / Jakarta EE 10; PostgreSQL 9.4 → 17
  • Keycloak SSO with separate client and staff realms
  • Asynchronous messaging for resilience
  • Apache Superset on a dedicated reporting database

Our role

Project leadership, architecture, full re-platforming, analytics and ongoing product ownership.

Microfinance · 2026In delivery

Chanuka Micro-Lending Platform

A complete lending platform for microfinance institutions, covering onboarding and credit scoring through disbursement, collections and portfolio-at-risk reporting. Integrated with KCB, M-Pesa and the credit bureau.

Financial technologyPayments & integrations
Project details

Chanuka gives a microfinance institution one system for the whole loan lifecycle. Customers are onboarded with KYC checks and credit-scored. Loans are originated and approved, disbursed to bank or M-Pesa, and repaid through automated collections. Field agents, savings and branch performance are managed in the same place.

It is built for growth and control. The platform is designed to scale to 18+ branches without re-architecture, with maker-checker approval on every sensitive action. Every money movement is signed, audited and reconciled against the bank.

Key capabilities

  • Onboarding and KYC
  • Credit scoring and loan origination
  • Disbursement to bank and M-Pesa
  • Repayments, collections and arrears
  • Savings and agent management
  • Portfolio-at-risk (PAR) reporting
  • Fraud and duplicate detection, bank reconciliation

Technology & integrations

  • KCB core banking, M-Pesa Daraja, credit reference bureau, SMS, WhatsApp
  • Modular Jakarta EE architecture, PostgreSQL primary/replica
  • Role-based web apps with Keycloak SSO
  • Signed API requests and append-only audit trails

Our role

Technical leadership, solution architecture and delivery.
